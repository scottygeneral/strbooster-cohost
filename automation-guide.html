Practical Guide

Choose your first automation with confidence

Start with a repetitive workflow that has a clear trigger and an observable outcome.

1. Map the current task

Write down what starts the task, the information it needs, the steps involved and who is responsible. Identify exceptions before choosing a tool.

2. Use what you already have

Check your PMS, CRM and existing integrations for relevant features. Avoid adding a subscription that duplicates something your current system can do.

3. Define the boundaries

Agree what the automation can change, when it must pause, and how it should escalate an exception. Keep human review where a mistake would materially affect a guest or booking.

4. Test a complete journey

Use test records to check triggers, timing, duplicate messages and failure handling. Confirm that the right person can see when a workflow fails.

5. Review the result

Compare the manual effort removed with the effort required to monitor and maintain the workflow. Keep the automation only if it improves the overall process.
