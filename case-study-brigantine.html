Client Case Study

A beach house with a clearer reason to book

How guest positioning, listing improvements and pricing work came together for a four-bedroom beach house in Brigantine.

Brigantine, New Jersey

The starting point

A four-bedroom beach house near the shore had a strong physical offering, but its listing was not communicating a compelling reason to book beyond the summer season. The project connected ideal guest positioning, listing presentation and pricing strategy.

What changed

Guest intelligence

Developed an ideal guest profile and additional weekday and shoulder-season guest segments to guide the positioning.

Listing copy and photo presentation

Reworked the title, description and supporting listing sections. Added descriptive captions, reviewed the photo sequence and enhanced lighting and presentation while keeping property details accurate.

Pricing and channel strategy

Reviewed PriceLabs occupancy-based adjustments, seasonal profiles and pricing offsets between Airbnb and Vrbo. The work considered rate strategy alongside the message shown to guests.

Reported performance

July 2025-January 2026 baseline vs. January 26-March 1, 2026. Different reporting windows; later results included below.
Reported metric Baseline Initial follow-up Later follow-up
Overall conversion 0.09% 0.53% 0.10%
First-page search impression rate 53.1% 63.9% 65.5%
Search-to-listing conversion 8.28% 15.77% 4.62%
Listing-to-booking conversion 1.04% 3.33% 2.13%

The initial comparison showed overall conversion rising by 0.44 percentage points, or approximately 489% relative to the rounded baseline. Search-to-listing and listing-to-booking conversion also increased. This was an observed early improvement, not a sustained sixfold result.

What the comparison also shows

The later dashboard, covering February 27-March 29, showed overall conversion at 0.10%, close to the original 0.09% baseline. Listing-to-booking conversion remained higher at 2.13% versus 1.04%, while search-to-listing conversion fell below the baseline. The follow-up demonstrates why each stage of the funnel should be monitored, rather than assuming the initial lift will persist.

Evidence and measurement context

Source: Airbnb dashboard screenshots reproduced in the supplied Brigantine case study. The listing was updated on January 30, 2026. The baseline spans July 2025-January 2026; the initial comparison covers January 26-March 1, including days before the update. The later window is February 27-March 29 and overlaps the initial window. These are unequal, partly overlapping periods, not a controlled experiment. Absolute traffic and booking counts were not supplied. Changes in demand, availability, pricing and guest mix may also affect conversion. The screenshots do not establish why Airbnb search placement changed.

The practical lesson

Guest targeting, presentation and pricing should work from a shared plan. Review the initial response, then keep checking the complete funnel as the booking season changes.
