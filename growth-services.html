Questions

Before you book a call

Do I need to change my PMS or pricing tool?

No. We build around the tools you already use. If a new tool would fill a clear gap, we explain why and agree it with you first.

What counts as a tracked direct booking?

A reservation made through your own website or booking engine and attributed to the system through its tracking, such as booking-engine handoffs, campaign links and enquiry records. We agree the tracking method with you before launch.

Is there a contract term?

The Owner Acquisition System has a three-month minimum. Other terms are confirmed in writing with your scope.

Do you build everything from scratch?

No, and that keeps setup faster and more affordable. We start from proven building blocks and configure them to your brand, your markets and your rules.

Will AI send messages without my approval?

Only where you allow it. You decide which replies run automatically, which campaigns need approval and when a conversation comes to your team.

Which system pays back first for you?

Bring your door count, your PMS and your growth goal. We will show you where direct revenue and new owners are being left on the table.
