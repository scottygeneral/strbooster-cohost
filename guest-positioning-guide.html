Practical Guide

Position your rental around the right guest

A practical starting point for identifying who your property serves best.

1. Start with the travel need

Describe why someone travels, who travels with them, and what a successful stay looks like. A purpose such as a quiet anniversary break is more useful than an age group alone.

2. Check the evidence

Look for repeated experiences in guest reviews and recurring questions in enquiries. Separate consistent strengths from occasional perks, and record where the property is a poor fit.

3. Choose the strongest promise

Connect a guest priority to something your property can reliably deliver. Make the promise specific and support it with accurate photos, amenities and location detail.

4. Apply it across the listing

Use the title and first photos to establish the experience. Expand the reasons to choose the stay in your description and answer practical concerns before they interrupt a booking.

5. Review by season

Different weeks can attract different travel needs. Revisit the guest profile when the reason to travel changes, while keeping the property facts consistent.
