What Is STR Booster?

STR Booster shares free growth systems for short-term rental hosts and property managers, built on Claude. You get a nine-step STR AI Journey and free Claude skills that turn each job, from your brand to pricing to guest messages, into a system Claude runs the same way every time. Nothing is for sale.

What is a growth system for a short-term rental?

A growth system is a repeatable way to do one job that grows the business, such as writing your listing, planning your marketing or checking your prices. Here, each system is a Claude skill that holds your facts and rules, so the work gets done the same way every time instead of from scratch.

Why is everything built on Claude?

Hosts already juggle too many apps. One assistant, set up once with your business facts, can do the work of many single-purpose tools. Claude projects keep each property’s facts together, and Claude skills follow the same steps every time.

Is STR Booster really free?

Yes. The journey, the prompts and every skill are free to download, with no sign-up. There are no services, no prices and no affiliate links. The optional email list sends the full playbook and new skills.

Where should I start?

Start with step 1 of the journey, the business brain. It takes about 45 minutes and gives Claude your goals, numbers and rules. Every later step reads it.
