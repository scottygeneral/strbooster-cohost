Questions

The details, up front.

Is this property management?+

No. STR Booster focuses on revenue strategy, positioning, guest intelligence and automation. Your listings, payouts, local vendors and property remain under your control.

Who is the Sprint best for?+

Established owners of roughly 1–5 premium vacation rentals who already have meaningful revenue and want professional performance without giving up control to a full-service manager.

Is this just an audit?+

No. Diagnosis is part of the work, but the Sprint is sold as implementation. We prioritize the changes most likely to improve performance and work through those changes during the 30-day engagement.

What does the US$2,750 include?+

The Sprint covers one primary property and includes revenue strategy, demand calendar, guest intelligence, listing conversion recommendations and implementation support, automation planning, repeat-booking opportunities and a 90-day growth roadmap. Additional properties can be scoped separately.

Do I need to change my PMS or pricing software?+

Usually not. The approach is tool-agnostic. We work with the systems you already have where practical and only recommend changes when there is a clear business reason.

What happens after 30 days?+

You can run the system yourself, continue with your existing team, or add optional ongoing growth support for revenue reviews, experimentation and optimization.
