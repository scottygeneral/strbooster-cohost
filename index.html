◎ The premise

Why STR Booster exists

The lodging was never the hard part. The complexity was. Too many logins, pricing spreadsheets, freelancers and a phone that never stops. We turn it into one system that works while you host.

The old way

Too many tools. Too many tabs. Freelancers to chase, prices to guess and messages at midnight.

The STR Booster way

One connected AI growth system, built for your business and supported by people who know hospitality.

◎ The growth stack

Four systems. One engine.

Start with the one that hurts most. Connect the rest when you are ready.

01

Automated marketing agents

Campaigns that write, launch and follow up.

AI agents plan your seasonal campaigns, write the emails and social posts, and keep your listing story sharp. You approve. They ship.

02

Revenue management & sales

Pricing and selling, finally connected.

Rates, local events and enquiries in one loop. We integrate with your pricing tool and put a sales agent on every enquiry, so demand turns into bookings.

03

Direct booking systems

Your channel. Your guests. Your margin.

A branded booking site, funnel and guest list you own. Bring past guests back without paying a commission every time they return.

04

Guest concierge

Five-star answers at any hour.

An AI concierge trained on your house information handles questions, requests and add-ons, then hands off to you when it matters.

For co-hosts & property managers

Need more properties? We build owner acquisition systems too.

Find, qualify and follow up with property owners automatically, so your portfolio grows while you host.
