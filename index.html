A few things
you might be asking.

Is this software or a service?+

A custom system with implementation and ongoing support. We agree what you need, configure the software and workflows, and help you put them to work. The scope and ongoing costs are agreed before we start.

Do I need to change my existing tools?+

We start with what you already use. Before agreeing a build, we confirm what can connect to your property management system, booking engine and other tools, and where a change would help.

Does AI take over my whole business?+

You choose the responsibilities and approval rules. We define the questions and tasks the system can handle, and how it hands over to you or your team. Cleaning, maintenance and on-site operations stay with your operating team.

Can you help with just one property?+

Yes. We scope the system around the opportunity and the work you want help with. That can mean improving one part of your guest journey or connecting several parts of a larger operation.

What happens on the discovery call?+

We spend 30 minutes discussing your properties, booking priorities and the tasks taking up your time. We identify where a custom system could help, then explain a sensible scope and next steps.
