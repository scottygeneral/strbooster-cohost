A few useful answers

Choose your starting point.

Is STR Booster right for one property?

Yes. The system is designed for individual owners, including owners of one established rental, who want better performance without turning hosting into another job.

Is the community free to join?

Yes. You can join Host Growth Hub for free and begin exploring the community.

Do I need to buy every service or app?

No. Choose the support that fits your priorities. Apps, implementation projects and ongoing services have separate scopes.

Do I have to replace my current tools?

We start with what you already use. We recommend changes when they address a clear business need, and agree any additional subscriptions before implementation.

Do you take over property management?

Your listings, payouts, vendors and operating decisions stay under your control. Our work focuses on revenue growth, positioning, marketing and systems.
