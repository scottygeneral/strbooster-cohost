The problem

Most owner pages end with “give us a call”.

Owners compare managers the way guests compare rentals. They want to know what their home could earn, why they should trust you and what happens next. A page of text and a phone number loses them to the manager who answers first.

Growth usually depends on referrals and the founder’s time. When things get busy, owner follow-up is the first thing to slip.

What’s included

A complete owner pipeline, built and run for you.

01 · Owner landing page

A page that answers owners’ real questions

What you manage, how you report, what you charge and why owners stay. Written for owners searching in your markets, and easy to use on a phone.

02 · Revenue estimate

A reason to raise their hand

Owners request an estimate of what their home could earn with you. Your team reviews it before it goes out, so every number is one you stand behind.

03 · Lead capture and qualification

Know which owners are worth a call

Each enquiry is captured in your CRM with the property, location, current setup and timeline, so your team only spends time on good fits.

04 · AI follow-up

Reply within minutes, not days

New enquiries get a helpful reply and a link to book a call straight away. Owners who are not ready yet get useful follow-up until they are.

05 · Nurture for owners and realtors

Stay top of mind with the people who refer

Regular, useful emails for self-managing owners and the realtors who sell investment homes in your area, so you are the first manager they think of.

06 · Monthly pipeline report

See what is working

Enquiries, booked calls, proposals and signed owners each month, with what we are changing next.

Questions

Owner acquisition questions

Who owns the leads?

You do. Owner enquiries and contact records live in your CRM, and they stay yours if you stop working with us.

Do I need a new website?

No. The owner page can sit on your current site or alongside it. We agree the best setup on the discovery call.

Who prepares the revenue estimates?

The estimate is built from your markets and your management model, and your team approves what goes to the owner. You stay in control of every number an owner sees.

What counts as a signed owner for the guarantee?

A new owner who signs a management agreement with you from a lead the system captured. The exact terms are confirmed in writing before you start.

Ready to stop waiting for referrals?

Bring your door count, your markets and your growth goal. We will show you what an owner pipeline could look like for your business.
