Be honest

Why you haven’t started yet.

You are not behind because you are not smart enough. You are tired of tools that promise a lot and add work. Fair. Here is how this is different.

“Not another app.”

You already juggle a PMS, a channel manager, a pricing tool and three inboxes. This journey adds no new app. It runs in Claude, one assistant you set up once.

“The margins are too thin to experiment.”

Everything here is free. Each step is small, takes minutes, and pays you back in time before you move on.

“I tried ChatGPT once and it was generic.”

Generic in, generic out. Step 1 gives Claude your facts, goals and voice, so every answer after that is about your business.

“I don’t want a robot talking to my guests.”

You stay in charge. Everything here drafts and checks. Refunds, complaints, safety and money always come back to a person.

The journey

Nine steps. Each one builds on the last.

Go in order. Open Claude (a free account is fine to start), create a project for your business, and paste each prompt in as you go.

  1. 1

    Step 1 of 9 · 45 minutes, once

    The brain: objectives and strategies

    AI with no goals and no facts gives random answers. Most hosts skip this step, then decide AI does not work.

    Do this

    1. Write down your goals for the next 12 months: revenue, occupancy, owners, time back.
    2. List your properties, who books them, the numbers you watch and the rules you never break.
    3. Create one Claude project called “Business brain” and add that document. Every later step starts here.
    Paste into Claude

    Interview me one question at a time to build the “business brain” for my short-term rental business: my goals for the next 12 months, my properties, my guests, the numbers I track, my voice and my rules. When we are done, write it as one clear document I can add to a Claude project. Do not invent anything I have not told you.

  2. 2

    Step 2 of 9 · 30 minutes

    Brand building

    If your listing sounds like every other listing, you compete on price. A clear brand is why a guest books you, and books direct next time.

    Do this

    1. Write your promise in one sentence: who you are for and why they will love staying with you.
    2. Pick three voice words, with what each one sounds like and what it never sounds like.
    3. Save it as a one-page brand guide in your business brain.
    Paste into Claude

    Using my business brain, draft a one-page brand guide for my rentals: the promise in one sentence, who it is for and who it is not for, three voice words with a do and a don’t example for each, and five phrases we use and five we avoid. Ask me before you invent any detail.

  3. 3

    Step 3 of 9 · 20 minutes

    Know your guest

    You cannot market to everyone. Your reviews already tell you who loves your place most, and why.

    Do this

    1. Paste your last 30 to 50 reviews into Claude with the Review Insight kit.
    2. Find your happiest guest: why they came, who they came with, what they praised.
    3. Add that guest profile to your business brain. It drives every step after this.
    Paste into Claude

    Here are my latest reviews. Find the guests who were happiest: their trip purpose, group type and season. List the top five things they praise, with exact short quotes, and anything that comes up more than once as a problem. Do not invent quotes or counts.

  4. 4

    Step 4 of 9 · 15 minutes per listing

    Optimize your listings

    Most listings are written for the host, not the guest. The title, first photo and first two lines decide the click.

    Do this

    1. Run the Listing Audit for the guest you found in step 3.
    2. Make the free fixes first: title, photo order, opening lines, amenity names.
    3. Note your views and bookings now, then run the audit again in 30 days.
    Paste into Claude

    Audit this listing for my target guest. Score the title, first photo and photo order, opening lines, fit signals, amenities, objection handling, proof and house rules from 1 to 5 with evidence. Then give me the top five fixes, free ones first, and three rewritten titles.

  5. 5

    Step 5 of 9 · 1 hour, then quarterly

    OTA distribution

    One channel is one algorithm change away from an empty calendar. Each channel has its own guests, limits and style.

    Do this

    1. Decide which channels matter for your guest: Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, your own site.
    2. Keep the facts identical everywhere, and adapt titles and descriptions to each channel.
    3. Check once a quarter that photos, rules and amenities still match across channels.
    Paste into Claude

    Here is my Airbnb listing and my guest profile. Adapt the title and description for Vrbo, Booking.com and my direct booking site. Respect each channel’s style and title limits, keep every fact identical, and list anything a channel asks for that I have not given you.

  6. 6

    Step 6 of 9 · 1 hour a month

    Marketing and sales

    No time for content, and past guests forgotten the day they check out. Your cheapest next booking is someone who already stayed.

    Do this

    1. Collect past-guest emails with consent, and send a short note each season.
    2. Turn real reviews and local events into posts. No stock phrases, no fake urgency.
    3. Answer every direct enquiry within five minutes, even with a holding reply.
    Paste into Claude

    Using my brand guide and guest profile, write a four-week plan: one past-guest email and two social posts a week, each built only from the reviews, photos and local events I give you. Keep my voice. No discounts unless I ask for one.

    Resources

    .md Monthly Marketing Plan kit Coming soon
  7. 7

    Step 7 of 9 · 20 minutes a week

    Revenue management

    Prices left on autopilot, or panic-discounted. A calm weekly check beats both.

    Do this

    1. Export the next 90 days from your pricing tool or PMS.
    2. Run the Weekly Pricing Check: pace by week, orphan nights, a short watch list.
    3. Make one change per date range and log it, so you learn what works for your home.
    Paste into Claude

    Here are my next 90 nights: date, booked, price and minimum stay, plus my typical lead time and floor price. Show pace by week as ahead, on track or behind, list orphan nights, and give me a watch list of three to five date ranges with one suggested action each. Never invent market data.

    .md Weekly Pricing Check Download ↓
  8. 8

    Step 8 of 9 · 15 minutes per owner

    Owner acquisition

    For property managers and co-hosts: owner pitches all sound the same. Show the owner their home and their guests, not your logo.

    Do this

    1. Before the first call, build a “who will book your home” report for that owner.
    2. Send it as a one-page PDF with one line of context.
    3. Use the call to walk through it together, with real numbers from your pricing tool.
    Paste into Claude

    Prepare a one-page report titled “Who will book [owner]’s home” for this property: the main guest it can win and why, the three most likely guest types with when they come and what wins them, best and slowest seasons, and three low-cost changes you would make first. Do not invent revenue figures or results.

    Resources

    .md “Who Will Book Your Home” Owner Report Download ↓
  9. 9

    Step 9 of 9 · 30 minutes to set up

    Guest experience

    The same twenty questions, every day, at every hour. Fast, warm, accurate replies are what five-star reviews are made of.

    Do this

    1. Set up the Guest Messaging Agent with your property facts.
    2. Draft every reply with it for two weeks, edit, and send yourself.
    3. Then automate only the safe questions: check-in, Wi-Fi, parking, directions. Keep money, complaints and safety with a person.
    Paste into Claude

    You are my guest messaging assistant. Using only the property facts I gave you, draft a reply to the guest message below in my voice, under 90 words. If it involves a refund, a complaint, damage, safety or anything not in my facts, start with “HOST NEEDED” and give me a holding reply instead.

Questions

Fair questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. The journey, the prompts, the resources and the emailed playbook are free, and there is nothing to buy. You do not need to sign up to use anything on this page.

Why Claude?

One assistant, set up once with your facts, replaces a pile of single-purpose apps. Projects keep each property’s facts together, and Skills let Claude follow the same steps every time.

Do I need a paid Claude plan?

You can start on the free plan. Projects, Skills and longer files work best on a paid Claude plan, which is what we use.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Each step is plain language and a prompt to copy. Each resource is a text file you upload. If you can write a guest message, you can use these.

Will the AI reply to my guests on its own?

Not unless you set it up to. Everything here drafts first, with clear rules for when a person must step in. Automate only what you have tested.
