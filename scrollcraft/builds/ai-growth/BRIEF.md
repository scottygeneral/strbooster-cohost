# STR Booster: AI growth systems

Self-authored implementation brief under the user's explicit instruction to proceed. The user supplied the direction and primary action; the choices below fill routine design gaps without an additional interview.

## Evidence and decisions

1. **Vibe.** User: "more simple and benefit lead easy to understand benefits for my icp" and "slighlty more saas style". Direction: clear, capable, calm, contemporary. Preserve the existing navy/green identity and Outfit typeface.
2. **Journey.** User wants custom AI growth/cohosting systems, ongoing support, and owner control. Authored order: immediate promise; three customer outcomes; an understandable worked example; control and support; existing client evidence; property-manager pathway; discovery call.
3. **Energy.** User: "more engaging with scroll motion". Restrained natural scrolling, one compact explanatory sequence, no scroll hijacking, no empty dwell, no autoplay media.
4. **Feeling and memory.** Clarity at the opening, recognition at the benefits, relief as the example connects, confidence through control/support and evidence, relevance for property managers, readiness at the call.
5. **Signature move.** A clearly labelled enquiry example progresses from question to useful answer to next step. Selecting a guest or owner scenario changes the content. A 'Host approval' path shows an exception reaching a human. The demonstration makes control visible rather than claiming universal autonomy.
6. **Range.** User requests slightly more SaaS style. Clean typography, functional interface diagrams, asymmetric rows, real links, no repeated feature-card grid, no decorative pseudo-dashboard or invented revenue figures.
7. **World.** User: "no more lifestyle images". Use semantic HTML, restrained SVG connectors and clear example content. No photography or generated footage is needed. Each section is independently understandable; desktop's one short pinned explanation becomes a normal-flow, button-controlled example on phones and under reduced motion.
8. **Assets.** Existing inline STR Booster mark, navy #0d1f2d, green #1a7f64, Outfit. Use Scrollcraft's engine unchanged under its MIT license. Existing case-study results retain dates and qualifications. No new testimonials or numerical performance claims.

## Structure and Scrollcraft adaptation

The user's simple SaaS/service brief takes precedence over a cinematic template. A short, navigable service explanation uses natural flow, one compact worked example and a booking close. The eight supplied grammars were considered: filmic/worldflight require unwanted imagery and excessive spectacle; editorial requires too much reading; a full live surface would imply a packaged application; typographic poster would hide the system; gallery would prioritise a catalogue; split stage would force a comparison; rhythmic cutlist would be too energetic. This is a documented simplification rather than a claim to reproduce those grammars.

Navigation links to benefits, the example and real client results. The close resolves in a discovery conversation. Ban full-page background media, long pins, scroll locks, fake application telemetry, default-hidden essential content, repeating grid cards and a free-report primary CTA.
