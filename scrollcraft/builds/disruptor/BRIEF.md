## Eight topics

1. **Vibe:** upstart, confident, cinematic tech. References (from the owner): NoGood (near-black canvas, wireframe/grid worlds, orbiting service labels, "No slop" voice) and WebitMD ("growth stack", "we'll build you a custom engine", all-caps statements).
2. **Journey:** promise and CTA; the services as a moving strip; the premise (complexity is the enemy); the stack, one system at a time; how it works in three moves; the reason it exists (guest experience); the founder; the close: Let's talk.
3. **Energy:** loud open, quiet premise, building through the stack, calm human middle, loudest at the close.
4. **Feeling curve** below; peak is the close.
5. **Signature move:** the premise section: a tangle-to-signal image whose green signal line draws across the page as the manifesto text lights up word by word, ending in the STR Booster mark.
6. **Range:** dark, bold, extended-feeling sans display (Outfit 800, tight), Fraunces italic for one accent phrase per heading. No gradient text.
7. **World:** distinct scenes on one dark canvas, cut by a light (bone) human section.
8. **Assets:** founder photo (`assets/founder-david.png`), brand guide, Higgsfield-generated renders and lifestyle photography (illustrative, not clients).
