# Brand: Songbird Acres

_Sample made with the Brand Guide skill (STR Booster playbook, step 2), November 2026. Demo property: review lines are demo text and the palette is a starting point._

- Promise: Eleven acres between you and noise.
- What it means: The quiet is the product. Every touchpoint protects it or gets out of its way.
- For: UGA parents in their 40s and 50s who come for games and campus weekends and want to sleep somewhere calm.
- Not for: Groups looking for a party base before or after the game; Anyone who wants to walk to downtown Athens; Events, showers or photo shoots; Guests who can't manage one flight of stairs
- Positioning: A private studio on eleven acres, 25 minutes from Sanford Stadium. Close enough for game day, far enough that the only thing you hear at night is the meadow.
- Values: Quiet, Unhurried, Honest, Neighborly
- Personality: A calm Georgia neighbor who waves from the porch, answers fast, and then leaves you be.
- Voice: Short, warm and plain. Specifics over adjectives: acres, minutes, bird names. Never pushy, never loud.
- Voice words:
  - Plain: sounds like "25 minutes to the stadium. Take Hwy 129 in on game day." Never "Conveniently located near all the action!"
  - Calm: sounds like "The patio lights come on at sunset. The deer come out a bit after." Never "Get ready for the ultimate game-day experience!!"
  - Specific: sounds like "Cardinals at the feeder around 7 am." Never "Abundant wildlife in a breathtaking natural setting."
- Words we use: eleven acres, 25 minutes, the meadow, game day, quiet, the patio, deer at sunrise, back roads
- Words we never use: cozy, nestled, stunning, oasis, hidden gem, serene, getaway, amazing, !!!
- Palette: ink #24382c, ground #f3efe3, accent #b5743b, support #5d7a5f (starting point, no logo yet)
- Display font: Georgia
- Decision filter:
  1. Does it serve the right guest? A UGA parent who wants to see the game and then sleep in quiet. Not a party group, not a crowd.
  2. Does it keep the promise? "Eleven acres between you and noise." Anything that adds noise, rush or worry at the promise's expense is wrong.
  3. Does it pull in the wrong guest? If a photo, line or channel would attract a party group or an event, it doesn't run.
  4. Is it specific? "Eleven acres" over "private". "25 minutes" over "close to Athens". "Cardinals at 7 am" over "wildlife".
