Frequently asked questions

What can be automated in a short-term rental business?

Common starting points are guest messaging, enquiry follow-up, review requests, CRM updates, social media scheduling and repetitive PMS tasks. The best first automation has a clear trigger and a result you can check.

Will AI reply to guests without my approval?

Only within limits you set. Each workflow has agreed permissions, checks and an escalation route to a person when a decision could affect a guest or a booking.

Do I need new software?

Usually not. We start with your existing PMS, CRM and integrations, and only recommend a new tool when it fills a clear gap.
