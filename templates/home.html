◎ The premise

Why STR Booster exists

Hosts do not need another app. Most of the work that slows a rental business down is writing, checking and following up. Claude can do a lot of that today, using the facts you already have. We build the systems and share them for free.

The old way

A new subscription for every problem. Logins nobody uses. The same guest questions answered by hand, and owner leads that wait days for a reply.

The STR Booster way

One assistant, Claude, set up with your property facts and a small set of tested systems. It drafts, checks and follows up. You decide what goes out.

◎ How it works

Three moves. Then Claude helps.

  1. 1

    Get the playbook

    Twelve short steps, from writing your property facts down once to answering every lead in five minutes. Free, no call needed.

  2. 2

    Set up Claude

    Create a Claude project for your property or portfolio, add your facts and save the kits as Skills. It takes about an hour.

  3. 3

    Run one system a week

    Start with guest messages, then listings, pricing and reviews. Draft first, check, then automate only what you have tested.

◎ Our edge: guest intel

Other tools find owners. We show them who will book their home.

Owners sign with the manager who understands their property. Guest Intel profiles the ideal guest for each home, who they are, why they travel and when they book,, and the free Claude kit turns it into a “Who will book your home” report you send before the first call.

You walk in with a plan, not a pitch. Once the home is yours, the same insight shapes the listing and the guest campaigns, so owners see that plan come true.
