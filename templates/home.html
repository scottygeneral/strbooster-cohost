A little clarity

Good
questions.

What exactly do you build?

Your rental's brand and booking funnel: guest positioning, a clear story, listing and website content, lead capture and relevant marketing follow-up. Strategy, implementation and ongoing marketing can be scoped separately.

Is this for one property?

Yes. We start with your property's strengths and the guests it serves best—not the size of your portfolio. Begin with your free Guest Intel snapshot.

Do I need to leave Airbnb or change my PMS?

No. Direct bookings complement your existing channels. We work alongside your property management system and operating team. We do not take over cleaning, maintenance or on-site guest support.

What is free, and what is paid?

Your Guest Intel snapshot is free. Brand projects, website and funnel implementation, ongoing marketing, software subscriptions and advertising spend are separate. Scope and costs are agreed before paid work begins.

Where does HighLevel fit?

HighLevel supplies marketing software. STR Booster supplies the vacation-rental strategy and help applying it. We are a HighLevel affiliate and may earn a commission when you subscribe through our referral link. We are not the developer of HighLevel.
