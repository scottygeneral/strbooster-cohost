A LITTLE CLARITY

Your next chapter,
without the guesswork.

What exactly does STR Booster do?

We help vacation rental owners build two connected things: a brand guests have a reason to choose, and a booking funnel that attracts interest, supports direct bookings and keeps guests coming back. Strategy, implementation and ongoing marketing can be scoped separately.

Is this for an owner with one property?

Yes. The starting point is your property, its strengths and the guests it serves best—not the size of your portfolio. Start with the free Guest Intel report to explore your guest positioning.

Is STR Booster a property manager or PMS?

No. Our specialty is branding and marketing. We work alongside your existing property management system and operating team; we do not take over cleaning, maintenance or on-site guest support.

Do I have to stop using Airbnb or Vrbo?

No. A direct-booking channel complements your existing channels. The goal is to build your own brand and guest relationships, not to remove a channel that works for your business.

What is free, and what is paid?

Your Guest Intel snapshot is free. Brand projects, website and funnel implementation, ongoing marketing, software subscriptions and any advertising spend are separate. Scope and costs are agreed before paid work begins.

Where does HighLevel fit?

HighLevel supplies marketing software. STR Booster supplies the vacation-rental strategy and help applying it. We are a HighLevel affiliate and may earn a commission when you subscribe through our referral link. That relationship does not make STR Booster the developer of HighLevel.
