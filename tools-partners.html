Hospitable focuses on hosting operations and guest communication. HighLevel supports CRM, lead capture, funnels and marketing follow-up. Start with your most pressing workflow need; you do not have to subscribe to both.

How do I receive the Hospitable discount?

Use our Hospitable referral link and check that the 25% discount for three months is shown or confirmed during signup. Review the provider’s eligible plans and price after the discount period before subscribing.

What is included with the HighLevel offer?

STR Booster provides free coaching, a customized HighLevel snapshot for short-term rental operations, and weekly group training and calls through support webinars when you subscribe through our referral link.

What is a HighLevel snapshot?

A snapshot is a preconfigured setup that gives you a starting point inside HighLevel. Our offer includes a version customized for short-term rental operations, with support to help you put it to work.

Is the HighLevel software free?

No. You pay HighLevel for your software subscription. The coaching, customized STR snapshot and weekly group support are the STR Booster bonuses attached to this referral offer.

I already have an account. Can I claim the offer?

Contact us before changing your subscription. We need to confirm referral eligibility for an existing account; do not create a duplicate account to try to claim an offer.
